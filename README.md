## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Ultimate Security** — Coinomi employs cryptographic encryption techniques, ensuring that your private keys never leave your device.
- **Privacy Protection** — All transactions are decoupled from your IP, guaranteeing your anonymity without transaction monitoring.
- **Integrated Exchanges** — Buy cryptocurrencies using a credit card and convert them into gift cards directly from the wallet.
- **Native SegWit Support** — Experience faster confirmations and lower exchange fees with native SegWit functionality.
- **Multiple Accounts** — Manage various accounts, each with distinct encryption settings, for better asset organization.

### Advanced Features:
- **Dynamic Transaction Fees** — Customize and adjust transaction fees to fit your needs.
- **UXTO Control** — Protect against dusting attacks with advanced control features.
- **Bitcoin Node Connection** — Connect directly to Bitcoin's own nodes for improved transaction reliability.
- **NFT and ERC271 Support** — Full compatibility with NFTs and ERC271 tokens.
- **Web3 and DApp Browser** — Explore the decentralized web with integrated support for DApps and WalletConnect.
